Privacy Policy

This app is used only by Smoke And Silver (@smokeandsilver.in) to automatically reply to comments on its own Instagram posts.

When someone comments on our posts, Instagram sends us the comment text, the commenter's username and IDs. We use this only to post a reply. We do not store, sell or share this data; it is processed in memory and may appear briefly in server logs, which are deleted automatically.

Data deletion: to ask for any data about you to be removed, delete your comment or contact us via Instagram DM at @smokeandsilver.in.
